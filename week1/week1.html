Author(s): Kehan Fan, Concept Ethan Chiu, Lead Developer Fabian Tjoe-A-On, Technical Direction, Music Nando Correa, UI Design 林遭遇, 2D Illustrator Henry Yen, 3D Artist Happy Ship, 3D Artist Daisy Zhang, Producer

The Monolith Project is a cinematic WebGL journey built with React Three Fiber. It weaves together modular shaders, layered particle systems, and a custom deferred renderer into a scroll-driven story that flows like a film. Every effect is modular. Every transition feels intentional.

The Monolith Project is an interactive, scroll-driven digital story that blends hand-drawn illustration, 3D worlds, sound, and cinematic transitions into a seamless, retro-futuristic experience. Inspired by artists like Moebius and films like 2001: A Space Odyssey, it transitions from 2D sketches to fully lit 3D scenes through custom shader-driven effects and perfectly timed motion. Built with Three.js, React Three Fiber, and GSAP, the project feels less like a website and more like a living short film.

I really like how this project uses warm colors and a flat illustration style to depict the universe. The whole experience feels like reading a dynamic picture book, with scenes transitioning from cave paintings and DNA double helixes, to books and computers, and finally to the smartphone in our hands. At key moments, it uses Tap and hold interactions, and the transitions between scenes, music, and illustrations are remarkably smooth. My favorite parts are the scene where the character dives downward in the middle, and the final part where the different small illustrations turn into text as you scroll.
